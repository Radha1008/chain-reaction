        ChainReaction

        An independent security-education project. Habit roles, narrative fragments, and named scenarios are hand-authored reference content assembled by a deterministic rules engine: not a live scan of any account, and not AI-generated security logic.

        Educational simulation: not professional security advice and not a guarantee of protection.

        How the simulation works

        This isn't an AI guessing what sounds scary. It's a deterministic rules engine modeled on how real attackers chain weaknesses together: the same thinking used in MITRE ATT&CK-style kill-chain analysis, pointed at everyday life instead of enterprise infrastructure.

        01

        You self-report

        Tick the habits that describe you, one area of your life at a time. No accounts, no real passwords, no personal data: just categories.

        02

        The engine checks for a chain

        Every habit is tagged by role (entry point, escalating weakness, or exposure signal) and matched against a library of real attack patterns.

        03

        You see the path

        Each step is pinned on a case board and joined by red string, from your first weak point to real impact. Tap any note to read it.

        04

        You get an unchain plan

        The fewest changes that close the most paths, each with a free official guide. Tick them off and watch your chain snap.
