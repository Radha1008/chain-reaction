How the simulation works

This isn't an AI guessing what sounds scary. It's a deterministic rules engine modeled on how real attackers chain weaknesses together: the same thinking used in MITRE ATT&CK-style kill-chain analysis, pointed at everyday life instead of enterprise infrastructure.

01

You self-report

Toggle the habits that describe you. No accounts, no real passwords, no personal data: just categories.

02

The engine checks for a chain

Every habit is tagged by role (entry point, escalating weakness, or exposure signal) and matched against a library of real attack patterns.

03

You see the path

A step-by-step walkthrough of how an attacker moves from your first weak point to real impact.

04

You see the fix

Every chain has a single weakest link. Fix that one habit and the whole path collapses.
